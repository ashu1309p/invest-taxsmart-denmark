ASK vs depot vs børneopsparing

Denmark hands a retail investor a few different "wrappers" to hold the same funds in - and the wrapper, not the fund, often decides the tax.

Tax year 2026 Every rate and amount below is for this tax year.

Three wrappers, one decision

A "wrapper" is just the type of account a fund sits in. The same world ETF can be taxed in different ways depending on where you hold it. None of these is universally best; the right one depends on whether you are investing for yourself or for a child, and how much. The Money Router on the Plan page puts them in a cheapest-tax order for a given target - this article explains what each one is first.

ASK - the low-rate wrapper

The Aktiesparekonto (ASK) is a special account taxed on a lager [mark-to-market] basis: each year you are taxed on the increase in value, whether you sold anything or not, and the tax is paid from the account. The rate is a flat 17% - lower than every other wrapper here, which is the whole point of it.

There is a deposit ceiling of 174,200 kr (2026): that limits how much you can pay in, not how large the account can grow. Only stocks and funds on the positivliste are allowed inside an ASK. Internal trades within the account do not use up the deposit ceiling, and a child can have one too.

Because the 17% applies symmetrically to good and bad years, the ASK is also a tidy home for a volatile "satellite" holding, where a flat rate handles the swings more evenly than an allowance-based account would.

The ordinary depot (frit depot)

An ordinary brokerage account - aktiedepot, often just "depot" - has no special wrapper rules: the fund you pick decides the tax. A fund on the positivliste is taxed as aktieindkomst [share income] at 27% up to 79,400 kr of yearly share income per person (158,800 kr combined for a married couple) and 42% above that. A fund off the list is taxed as kapitalindkomst [capital income] at roughly 37-42%. Which list a fund is on is the subject of its own explainer.

When the tax bill lands depends on what you hold. Investment funds are taxed yearly on the change in value (lager), the same mechanism as the ASK. Single stocks in a depot are realisation-taxed instead: nothing is due until you actually sell, so you control the timing. The depot has no ceiling, which makes it the adult workhorse for everything beyond the ASK.

Børneopsparing - the child wrapper

Børneopsparing [child savings] is the cheapest tax home in Denmark: 0% on every krone of return, forever. The trade-off is size and access. You can deposit at most 6,000 kr per year and 72,000 kr in total, there is one per child, it is opened before age 14, and it is locked until it pays out at some point between ages 14 and 21. It can hold funds and stocks at some banks, and the positivliste does not matter inside it.

It helps to compare it with simply investing in the child's own depot. There, an off-list accumulating fund is kapitalindkomst, and the child's personfradrag [tax-free allowance] of 54,100 kr (2026) absorbs that yearly gain - so it is effectively 0% until the gain grows past the allowance, with no lock and no contribution cap. That is why families typically fill the tiny, locked-but-untaxed børneopsparing first, then keep going in the child's depot using the frikort.

At a glance

ASK Ordinary depot Børneopsparing
Who it's for Adults wanting the lowest rate on listed funds; children too. Anyone, for anything beyond the other wrappers. One per child, opened by family.
How gains are taxed Flat 17% a year (lager). On-list 27/42%; off-list ~37-42%. 0% on all returns.
Key limit Deposit ceiling 174,200 kr. No ceiling. 6,000 kr/yr, 72,000 kr lifetime.
Main catch Positivliste funds and stocks only. The fund decides the tax; off-list costs adults more. Locked until paid out at age 14-21.

Key takeaways

  • ASK: a flat 17% lager wrapper for listed funds, with a 174,200 kr (2026) deposit ceiling - usually an adult's cheapest home for stock funds.
  • Ordinary depot: no ceiling, but the fund decides the tax - on-list aktieindkomst 27/42% vs off-list kapitalindkomst ~37-42%.
  • Børneopsparing: 0% forever, but tiny (6,000 kr/yr, 72,000 kr lifetime) and locked until age 14-21.
  • A child's own depot with an off-list fund uses the 54,100 kr (2026) personfradrag to reach near-0% with no lock or cap.
