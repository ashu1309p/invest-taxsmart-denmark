Who made this

I'm Ashutosh Pathak, a technology and delivery professional based in Copenhagen. I've spent close to two decades building and shipping complex software platforms, and I built this site the same way I approach that work: carefully, with every number checked against the source and every claim something I could point to.

It started as my own problem. Like a lot of people investing in Denmark, I kept re-deriving the same questions (which account to use, whether a fund sits on SKAT's positivliste, how gifts to children are taxed) from scattered, often Danish-only sources. I wanted one plain place that just showed the answer, in English and Dansk. So I made it.

A few honest things: this is a personal, non-commercial project. There are no ads, no login, and nothing for sale. I'm not a tax advisor, accountant, or lawyer; I'm an investor who reads the rules carefully. Everything here is educational, every figure is dated to its tax year, and every page points you back to skat.dk so you can verify it yourself.

What this site assumes (and what it doesn't)

Every amount on this site is tagged to a single tax year. Right now that is tax year 2026, taken from one place in the code (TAX_YEAR_CONFIG), so the Plan and Play tools and these articles can never quietly disagree.

The figures are SKAT and Skatteministeriet (SKM) published values for that year. Indexed amounts such as allowances and thresholds are adjusted every January, so a number that is correct this year can change next year.

Whether a fund's gains count as share income or capital income depends on SKAT's positivliste. What this site refers to is a periodic snapshot of that official list, and a snapshot can lag a change by a while, so confirm the current list before you act.

The simulators illustrate generic scenarios to show how the rules interact. They are not personalized advice, they do not know your situation, and rates or thresholds can change with new law.

Sources & legal references

Everything here traces back to official sources. Where a page makes a specific claim, it links the exact reference.

  • Positivliste (ABIS list) - skat.dk
  • Parent attribution - Den juridiske vejledning C.A.9.3 (kildeskatteloven § 5, stk. 2)
  • Børneopsparing - Den juridiske vejledning C.A.10.4.1.4
  • Aktiesparekonto - skat.dk (aktiesparekontoloven)
  • Gift caps - boafgiftsloven § 22
  • Annual indexed amounts - Skatteministeriet satser
