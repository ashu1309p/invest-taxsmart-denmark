Grandparents giving: 0

⚠ The interest trap (parent attribution) Interest and dividends on money gifted by a parent are taxed at the PARENT until the year the child turns 18 - and banks do not report this automatically; you must self-declare. Annual value gains on accumulating fund units are taxed at the child instead. That is why children's gifted savings go into accumulating funds, never idle cash or distributing funds. kildeskatteloven § 5, stk. 2 · SKM2024.25.SR · info.skat.dk C.A.9.3
🎁 The grandparent exception Gifts from grandparents are NOT covered by parent attribution: all returns are taxed at the child immediately. Same annual cap per giver applies.

Gavebrev [gift letter] checklist

No notary or special form is required, but conditions only count if they exist at or before the gift - never retroactively.

  • One letter per giver, per child, per year - signed BEFORE the first transfer
  • Transfer from the giver's OWN account (never a joint account), labelled with giver + recipient + year
  • Consider a fuldstændigt særeje [full separate property] clause - protects against the child's future divorce, and the child cannot undo it
  • Båndlæggelse [lock-up until 21/25] requires a godkendt forvaltningsafdeling - ordinary brokers hand full control to the child at 18
  • Two adult, non-beneficiary witnesses with name + address (proves the date)
  • Scan it and email it to yourself - a timestamp you cannot lose
For gavebreve with conditions, båndlæggelse, or large amounts: have an advokat [lawyer] or revisor [accountant] review before you transfer. This page teaches the mechanics - it cannot check your situation.
